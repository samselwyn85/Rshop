About us.

Our kaupapa is to provide our clients with answers to their questions. It’s that simple.

Whether it is understanding the impact of a set of government policies on a specific group within a population, knowing whether a data collection instrument is meeting the needs of end users or any number of other questions that require our qualitative and/or quantitative expertise, you can trust us to provide reliable data in agreed timeframes.

Our purpose-built indicators are designed to provide you with the confidence to make business decisions using accurate information. We use whatever measures necessary to get the job done resulting in robust outcomes.

By blending the value of statistics with interrogative analysis and a range of other techniques, we are able to ensure an end product which tells a coherent story. This enables us to stand behind the work we do so you can be on the front foot.

Gina’s passion is in helping an organisation creatively and collaboratively move toward being a more inclusive environment where everyone, despite their background, feels good about offering their best, and where clients feel safe about bringing their business. In training, Gina is a dab hand at saying difficult and hard things in palatable yet straightforward and liberating ways.

Services.

Our kaupapa is to provide our clients with answers to their questions. It’s that simple.

  • We identify the constituent parts of your problem and arrange those elements into functional groups. This enables us to either isolate the crux of the issue or build a picture of the relationships which are causing the chain reaction. We then present you with the substance you are after in the format you’d prefer. Whether the research is ethnographic (analysing a group of people), narrative (tells the story that emerges from a group of documents), phenomenological (relates to people’s aggregate experience with something), grounded (used to test a hypothesis or theory) or a case study (focuses on an individual case, experience or phenomenon), we do it.

  • We analyse situations or events, especially the financial market, populations of people or animals, political indicators and trade figures, by means of complex mathematical and statistical modelling. This enables us to quantify past phenomena, explain current trends and to some extent predict future probabilities. If it can be counted, you can count on us to do it.

  • We follow a systematic process for determining and addressing needs, or gaps between current conditions and desired conditions or wants. We gather appropriate and sufficient data to inform the process of developing an effective product that will address the needs and wants of your end-users. If you want to make sure that those who use what you produce are happy with it and especially how you could make them even happier, we can tell you that.

  • You can be as involved or uninvolved as you like in this process, but whatever the case, we make sure that you are informed every step of the way. Once we’re agreed on the survey objectives, resources and time constraints, we can provide you with a product that is tailored to meet your needs right from the survey method to be used through the critical question composition phase to evaluation of the responses. All of our questionnaires are tested before going into the field so you can rest assured that it is fit for purpose.

  • We collect information from all the relevant sources to find answers to your research problem by testing hypotheses and evaluating the outcomes. We use both primary and secondary data collection methods and apply both or either depending on the nature of the question you want answered. Where sources don’t already exist, we create them!

  • Once we’ve agreed on the terms of reference, we can take care of the rest. Whether you already have all of the information and you just need it sensibly compiled or whether you only have a title and nothing else, we can take the headache out of getting that final product ready for presentation to any audience. No job is too big or too small so go ahead and let us provide a product that meets your specifications.

  • Our focus groups are small, but demographically diverse groups of people. We study and record participants’ reactions and responses to everything from market research into new products or political analysis of policy preferences to the effectiveness of lines of questioning for wider research purposes. Through guided or open discussions about a new product or something else to determine the reactions that can be expected from a larger population, we are able to accurately predict the effectiveness of a product, policy or research tool so that you don’t waste a lot of energy and resource on a white elephant.

  • We provide instruction to achieve cultural competency and the ability to appreciate and interpret accurately other cultures. From in-depth workshops on Te Tiriti o Waitangi through engagement exercises in systemic racism to realistic workplace problem-solving in the bi- and multi-cultural space, you will find our offerings both informative and practical. Prepare to be both confronted and inspired as we expose the unsaid and give you a legitimate New Zealand education.

  • This value-added product allows us to take data your business or organisation already has and use it to best effect. Whether it is how to increase membership, vote-share, sales, response rates or subscriptions, let us show you the power of the information you’ve already collected by exploiting its salient features. We can build a story from the statistics that will lead to a happy ending.

  • We undertake both in-depth face to face and superficial phone polling. Whether it be targeted or representative, we can provide attitudinal data from a range of subjective measures using both simple and complex scales. Let our polls fill the holes in your political puzzle.
